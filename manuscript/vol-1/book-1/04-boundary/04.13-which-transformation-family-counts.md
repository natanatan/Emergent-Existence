# 4.13 Which Transformation Family Counts?

Retention is relative to a regime of variation. A distinction robust under one family may collapse under another. The chapter exposes this relativity rather than treating it as an embarrassment.

At this stage 𝔗\* is selected by the diagnostic question. Later physics must earn a principled transformation family from dynamics, coupling, coarse-graining, symmetry, or empirical constraint. If the mature theory requires an irreducibly primitive physical transformation class in addition to Difference and 𝒰, then “two foundational commitments so far” must be revised upward.

This is also why the internality standard differs between Chapters 3 and 4 without inconsistency. 𝒰 is constitutive of Distinction and therefore must belong to the modeled structure. 𝔗<sub>test</sub> is a modal probe of robustness and therefore may remain metalanguage until a later physical theory identifies which probes correspond to realized transformations.
