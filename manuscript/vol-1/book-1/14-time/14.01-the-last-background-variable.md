# 14.1 The Last Background Variable

Space ended with a coordinated domain of localization, adjacency, extension, and neighboring local reference frames. It did not tell those frames how long anything takes. That omission is deliberate. If duration had already been hidden inside retention, recursion, propagation, update, or the ordering of the earlier chapters, Time would not be an emergence at all. It would be a background parameter disguised as bookkeeping.

Time is therefore the final major background structure Book I must confront. The problem is not to redescribe familiar clock time in relational vocabulary. The problem is to identify the weakest inherited structure from which a physically interpretable temporal order could become possible without assuming seconds, trajectories through a temporal dimension, or a universal before-and-after relation at the outset.

The chapter begins from a fact already established by Boundary and carried forward by later chapters: retained distinctions can constrain admissible successor structure, yielding retained-dependency precedence ≺. That fact is weaker than physical time. In particular, Boundary does not guarantee that ≺ is transitive, acyclic, total, or asymmetric. Time must therefore earn any stronger ordering property it uses.
