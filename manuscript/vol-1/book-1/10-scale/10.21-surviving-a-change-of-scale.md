# 10.21 Surviving a Change of Scale

With a scale transformation available, we can ask whether a feature survives it. Scale invariance means that some identified organization is preserved when the scale relation is applied. This is a statement about what stays the same rather than about how big anything is. Self-similarity is the stronger case, where the organization at one level reproduces the organization at another under the comparison. It can be stated abstractly, without fractals and without a metric.

▪ scale invariance ≠ geometric self-similarity

The familiar fractal reading needs a metric, which Distance earns; it needs no embedding, since fractal dimension is defined intrinsically on any metric domain. Geometry teaches it (12.19). What we have here is the abstract form the geometric version will instantiate wherever measured scaling earns it.
