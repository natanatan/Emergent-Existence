# 9.11 Lumpability as a Later Probabilistic Analogue

Closure’s addressability condition has a recognized analogue in stochastic dynamics. Kemeny and Snell’s theory of lumpability asks when a Markov chain projected onto blocks of states remains a well-defined Markov chain. The probabilistic theorem is richer than anything available here, since it presupposes transition probabilities, but its structural lesson holds. Coarse-graining is dynamically legitimate only when the lower-order distinctions being ignored do not create ambiguous higher-order evolution.

▪ well-defined quotient dynamics now; probabilistic lumpability later, if probability is earned

Coarse-graining can also be mathematically useful without making every coarse-grained category ontologically real. The additional claim of higher-order autonomy requires stability and well-defined dynamics, not merely a partition.
