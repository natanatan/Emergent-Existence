# Questions Opened by This Framework

- Can phase be derived from the cycles earned in Return, without importing complex amplitudes?
- Can admissible transformation 𝒰 be shown to allow continuous, reversible transformation between definite states, so that reconstruction theorems apply?
- Is Planck's constant derived from the smallest stable relational cycle, or is it an input?
- What measure captures how incompatible two local conditionings of the field are, and at what value does retention occur?
- Where does that threshold fall relative to existing collapse-model bounds, and what experiment would test it next?
- Can a local excitation reshape the field while conserving energy and the other conserved quantities?
- What determines the distribution of starting points for excitations across a detector, and does it reproduce the Born rule?
- Can one update law produce both the interference pattern and the single localized record?
- How does coupling a detector at the slits change the relation between the field's parts, and does that reproduce every observed dependence on the apparatus?
- Does disordered locality reproduce Bell correlations exactly, without signaling and with Lorentz invariance intact?
- Can the localization mechanism be derived as the same conditioning whose large-scale expression is gravity, and why would their strengths differ by about 10³⁶?
- Can classical geometry, classical probability and stable records all be recovered as large-scale limits of one dynamics?
- What mathematical condition makes an emitter-field-detector interaction consistent at both ends, and does it select exactly one retained outcome?
- Does Chapter 14's account of time imply that an unretained interaction has no internal order, or is it only compatible with it?
- Does the consistency condition forbid controllable signaling into the past in every arrangement, including delayed choice and Maudlin's?
- How does a closure that involves no propagation coarse-grain into gravity that travels at the speed of light?
- Is the observational frame W_obs ultimately the same as the initial frame W_0?
