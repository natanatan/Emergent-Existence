# 2.11 Minimal Contrast Is Not Yet Relational Organization

The primitive dyad is relational in the minimal sense that its poles are characterized together. It is not yet what the later Relation chapter means by relational organization.

This distinction prevents a vocabulary trap. “Relation” in ordinary metaphysics can name any connection between terms; “Relation” as a later chapter title names the emergence of mutually constraining organization among multiple retained relations.
