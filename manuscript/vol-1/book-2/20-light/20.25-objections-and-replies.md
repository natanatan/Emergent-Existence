# 20.25 Objections and Replies

**Objection 1: Why should light be understood as a self-maintaining open recursive closure at all?**

**Reply.** The proposal earns its place only if one organization can jointly account for continuous propagation, phase recurrence, polarization, interference, momentum, redirection and identity through transformation. Open closure provides one mechanism connecting these: the update cycle preserves identity, and the hand-off preserves propagation. The final judgment depends on whether a formal model recovers the measured relations.

**Objection 2: What justifies treating the recursive return's angular momentum as a real degree of freedom rather than a picture?**

**Reply.** $L_{\text{closure}}$ is retained only if it does independent work: if it contributes to frequency, polarization, coupling or interference in a way that cannot be absorbed into the handedness χ, and if it has some measurable consequence. 20.18 shows the constraint it must meet. If it does no independent work, it should be removed rather than protected as imagery.

**Objection 3: Wave theory already describes frequency and amplitude. What is gained?**

**Reply.** Wave theory describes them with extraordinary predictive success, but predictive success does not show that the description captures the mechanism producing them. This proposal asks what organized relational structure must exist for frequency, amplitude and polarization to arise and transform as they do. It adds value only if it recovers the predictions of wave theory and also identifies that mechanism.

$$\text{predictive adequacy} \neq \text{mechanistic completeness}$$

**Objection 4: A spread-out coherence envelope seems incompatible with the discrete way light is detected.**

**Reply.** Spread-out propagation and discrete detection answer different questions. The envelope describes the organization sustaining propagation. Detection occurs when an interaction closes that excitation at one place and resolves a unit of energy there. Nothing requires the preceding propagation to have been a point travelling along a line.

**Objection 5: An active spatial medium is the nineteenth-century ether again, which Michelson and Morley ruled out.**

**Reply.** Michelson and Morley ruled out a medium whose motion relative to Earth would show up in light's measured speed. The medium proposed here is not a substance inside space; it is the continually updated relational organization we call space. Observers, clocks and rulers are constituted within the same update regime, so any viable dynamics must recover the observed invariance of local light-speed measurements (20.12, 20.19). Whether the underlying relational geometry possesses any globally privileged frame remains unresolved. A cosmologically preferred frame need not be a fundamental frame of the spatial substrate, because the large-scale organization and smoothness of spatial geometry are themselves evolved relational conditions. The relatively smooth present geometry is a contingent state and should not be projected backward as a timeless fundamental structure. If explicit dynamics produced a locally detectable preferred frame where none is observed, the model would fail.
