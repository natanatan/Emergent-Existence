# The Hard Questions

The story of this chapter is now told: a field whose alternatives combine, a measurement that is a physical act, a threshold where incompatible geometries force one outcome, and a world of relations that is more connected than the space it produces. Everything above was stated as directly as the framework allows, so that its shape could be seen whole.

The rest of the chapter asks what a careful reader should ask next. What exactly do experiments establish, and what do they leave open? What sets the threshold, and can it be measured? Where does Planck's constant come from? Why do the dots land with the frequencies they do? Each of the following sections takes up one such question, says what the framework has and has not yet earned, and states what would count against it.
