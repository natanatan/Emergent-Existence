# 11.14 Distance Without an Embedding

The relational domain also need not sit inside a larger space. If d(A,B) is determined entirely by relations internal to the domain, then the distance is intrinsic to that relational organization.

▪ intrinsic distance ≠ embedding distance

An embedding tells us how a structure sits inside something else. An intrinsic metric tells us how positions within the structure relate to one another. Volume I needs the first before it can legitimately discuss the second.
