# 4.6 A Preservation Predicate

Return later reserves Ret(A<sub>F</sub>; ·) for re-entry into the identity class of an organizational anchor A<sub>F</sub>. Boundary therefore uses a different predicate. For T ∈ 𝔗<sub>test</sub>, define Pres<sub>W</sub>(a,b;T) by the condition that the images of the two poles remain non-equivalent as resolved by W:

▪ Pres<sub>W</sub>(a,b;T) iff T(a) ≁<sub>W</sub> T(b)

Equivalently, the transformed a/b distinction remains represented in Res<sub>W</sub> of the target structure. Pres<sub>W</sub> is truth-valued. It is not an information quantity.

The definition is deliberately frame-relative. A transformation can preserve a distinction for one resolving frame and erase it for another.
