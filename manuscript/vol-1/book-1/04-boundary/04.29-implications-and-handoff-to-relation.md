# 4.29 Implications and Handoff to Relation

Relation now inherits substantially more than a retained pair, but less than an Ω-centered architecture would assume. It receives symmetric retained distinctions r<sub>ij</sub>; resolving frames W; admissible possibility sets 𝒫; retained-dependency precedence ≺; and the identity-equivalence scaffold ~<sub>F</sub>. It does not receive a privileged anchor.

The derivational chain is now:

▪ Difference ⇝ admissible succession ⇝ reciprocal Distinction ⇝ retention ⇝ dependency ⇝ mutual constraint

Relation must show how several retained distinctions cease to vary independently and become an integrated organization. It may use W, 𝒫, ≺ and ~<sub>F</sub> because Boundary has now introduced them. If Relation still requires Ω as an anchor, that requirement must be removed, reformulated as a criterion, or explicitly deferred until Closure can attempt to earn endogenous reference.

Handoff to Relation. When several retained distinctions coexist and constrain one another’s admissible possibilities, what additional condition turns them from a collection into a persistent relational organization?

*A distinction can now survive change, and what survives can constrain what follows.*

Questions Opened by This Framework

What physically selects the transformation family under which a distinction should count as retained?

Can frame-relative resolvability be physically instantiated without an observer and without a metric?

Which properties of retained-dependency precedence are necessary before causal or temporal interpretation becomes warranted?

Can higher-order identity be built from ~<sub>F</sub> and mutual constraint without any privileged anchor?

Can Closure subsequently earn an endogenous reference condition without undoing exchange symmetry at the foundation?

Can quantitative information theory be recovered from consequential retained distinctions rather than imported as a separate primitive?

What computational test would show that the sequence 𝒰 ⇝ retention ⇝ 𝒫 ⇝ ≺ fails to add explanatory content beyond an ordinary transition system?
