# 10.12 The Same Comparison Across Levels

Recursive building gives us more than one magnitude to compare. If a rule builds each level from those before it, each level has a magnitude, and consecutive levels have a comparison. What we are now able to ask. This we could not ask in Formalism, is whether that comparison stays the same as the building proceeds.

▪ Cmp(O<sub>n+1</sub>, O<sub>n</sub>) for successive levels of one recursive organization

This is where perspective changes: we are no longer looking at one organization and asking its size. However, standing between levels and asking whether the relation between them holds steady.
