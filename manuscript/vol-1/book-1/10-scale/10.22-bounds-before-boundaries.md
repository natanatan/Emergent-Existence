# 10.22 Bounds Before Boundaries

Comparative magnitude lets us state a bound without stating a size. An organization can be no larger than a standard, or no smaller, and both are pairings rather than measurements. This matters for Boundary’s boundary work, which defined separability without any notion of extent. A bound in this sense is a comparison, and a boundary in Boundary’s sense is a preserved non-equivalence. They are different things that English names alike.

▪ relational bound ≠ geometric boundary
