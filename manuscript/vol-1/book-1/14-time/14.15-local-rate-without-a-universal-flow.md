# 14.15 Local Rate Without a Universal Flow

If clocks are relational subsystems, their apparent rates may vary with local organization. Nothing in the derivation requires a single metaphysical flow that every subsystem samples identically. What must remain stable is the rule by which rates are compared once the relevant coupling conditions are specified.

This distinction will matter later. A physical theory may permit local clock rates to differ while preserving cross-frame laws that relate those differences. Curvature, acceleration, environmental coupling, or deeper relational structure could affect realized clock behavior without making the governing relation arbitrary.

Book I does not derive the relativistic law governing such variation. It only avoids foreclosing the possibility by refusing to identify time with a single universal succession parameter.
