# 23.24 What Would Count Against the Claim

The Claim would be directly undermined if a single localized outcome were shown to arise without any consequential coupling between detector and field; if no feedback law can conserve the relevant quantities while producing localization; or if superpositions were shown to persist, without decoherence, at scales where any defined incompatibility threshold would have forced retention.

The physical-threshold reading would fail if every version of the threshold consistent with the relational dynamics lies in a region already excluded by collapse-model experiments.

The account of entanglement would fail if disordered locality cannot reproduce Bell correlations exactly, within Tsirelson's bound and without signaling, while keeping Lorentz invariance.

The unification conjecture would fail if the conditioning that produces localization cannot be related to the conditioning from which gravity emerges in Chapter 22, or if deriving the small-scale mechanism destroys the large-scale agreement with general relativity.
