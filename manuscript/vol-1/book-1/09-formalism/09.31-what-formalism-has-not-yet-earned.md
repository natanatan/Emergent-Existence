# 9.31 What Formalism Has Not Yet Earned

Metric distance, length, duration, speed, angle or norm.

Vector-space structure, inner products, orthogonality or basis vectors.

A physical topology, manifold, continuum or coordinate chart.

Probability weights, stochastic dynamics or quantum amplitudes.

Physical causality or a causal cone.

A Hamiltonian, action, energy or field equation.

A claim that reality literally is a graph, matrix, simplex, category, automaton or quotient algebra.

A universal claim that every emergent entity must be a quotient structure.

A proof that binary relations suffice for every higher-order constraint.

A proof that physical or spatial dimensionality is d<sub>R</sub>, or that d<sub>R</sub> must equal a matroid rank in every realization.

A proof that determinism is fundamental, or that indeterminism is.

A physical interpretation of fixed points, attractors or recurrence periods.

Numerical scale, proportion or measurement units, and physical dimensionality.

We earn something narrower but foundational. A common formal language in which the earlier ontology can be expressed, transformed and quotiented without silently importing later physical structure.
