# 7.9 Symmetry Breaking Without Hard-Coding Direction

The strongest objection to derived orientation is that direction must already be hidden in admissible transformation. Meeting it requires separating three questions: whether symmetry is absolute, whether asymmetry can become effective, and whether an effective asymmetry is retained.

If an initial configuration were absolutely symmetric, admissible transformation perfectly symmetry-preserving, and the successor unique, symmetry would remain invariant. The third condition is substantive. Chapter 3 already types 𝒰 as a transition relation rather than a generally single-valued function, so multiple successors may be admissible without probability having been introduced. Equivariance constrains symmetry-related branches; it need not force a unique symmetric successor. Established symmetry-breaking theory likewise distinguishes symmetry of laws from symmetry of particular states or solution branches (Bernstein, 1974; Michel, 1980; Brading, Castellani and Teh, 2023).

This framework does not need to claim that a perfectly homogeneous metaphysical state chooses a direction without further structure. This is because 1.5’s origin leaves lower-order variation unspecified rather than denied. The question becomes whether some variations participate in organizations that survive higher-order resolution and closure. The proposed mechanism is differential persistence:

▪ unresolved variation ⇝ effective asymmetry ⇝ differential persistence ⇝ closed higher-order organization ⇝ orientation

A perturbation does not directly create orientation. Most perturbations may disappear, cancel or remain below the higher-order resolving threshold. A perturbation becomes relevant only when its consequences are incorporated into a higher-order dependency structure that persists and remains consequential to return. This is a restrained form of structural selection. Among dynamically accessible asymmetric configurations, those compatible with maintaining or recovering a higher-order identity persist under a wider class of admissible transformations than those that destroy it. “Selected” implies no agency, optimization, probability or biological fitness. It means differential persistence under the already declared dynamics and perturbation class.
