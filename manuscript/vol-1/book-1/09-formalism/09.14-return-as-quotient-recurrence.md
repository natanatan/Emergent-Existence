# 9.14 Return as Quotient Recurrence

Return can now be restated economically in the formal carrier. Let O<sub>F</sub> = \[Σ0\]<sub>~F</sub> be the higher-order identity whose Closure-earned organizational reference role is A<sub>F</sub>. A later configuration Σ<sub>k</sub> may differ from Σ0 while re-entering O<sub>F</sub> after genuine departure.

▪ \[Σ₀\]<sub>F</sub> = \[Σ<sub>k</sub>\]<sub>F</sub>, while Σ₀ ≠ Σ<sub>k</sub>

At the quotient level, a return-capable trajectory may be represented as O<sub>F</sub> 𝒰̄ … 𝒰̄ O<sub>F</sub>. This does not mean the anchor role A<sub>F</sub> is itself a state token that loops. A<sub>F</sub> is the reference role of O<sub>F</sub>; the recurrence is re-entry of distinct lower-order realizations into the same identity class.

▪ identity-type recurrence can coexist with token-level acyclicity
