# 13.22 Spatial Objectivity Is Cross-Frame Invariance

Different probing frames may represent the same spatial organization differently. Let Sp<sub>F</sub> = Π<sub>F</sub>(I<sub>F</sub>(R)) denote the spatial organization resolved through interaction I<sub>F</sub> and frame-relative resolution map Π<sub>F</sub>.

We need not require Sp<sub>F1</sub> = Sp<sub>F2</sub> at the level of raw representation. What matters is whether declared spatial invariants agree across admissible heterogeneous probes.

H(Sp<sub>F1</sub>) ~ H(Sp<sub>F2</sub>) ~ …

▪ spatial objectivity = cross-frame stability of coordinated localization invariants

This guards against two opposite errors: treating frame-dependent representation as evidence that space itself is subjective, and treating one frame’s representation as ontologically exhaustive. Neither follows.
