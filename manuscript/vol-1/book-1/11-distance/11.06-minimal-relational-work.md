# 11.6 Minimal Relational Work

There is a useful broader interpretation. We may describe L(κ) as the comparative burden of structure required to connect two positions, provided “work” is understood pre-physically and implies no traversal.

Relational work is the comparative burden of relational structure required to connect one admissible position to another.

▪ distance = minimum relational work of connection

Minimum relational mediation is formally sufficient at this stage. Relational work remains an interpretive analogy, and the chapter does not promote it into a minimality law about how organizations form.
