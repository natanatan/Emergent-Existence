# 13.24 Counterfactual Distinctions

The chapter depends on keeping neighboring concepts apart. Each distinction below functions as a counterfactual control: if the right-hand concept has been assumed merely because the left-hand concept exists, the derivation has overreached.

| **Distinction A** | **Distinction B** | **Why the distinction matters** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| geometry | space | Geometric coherence alone does not establish physical localization consequence. |
| coordination | coordinates | Coordination constitutes mutual position; coordinates label or represent it. |
| joint resolvability | simultaneity | Joint configuration precedes metric Time. |
| edge | spatial neighbor | Primitive connectivity cannot carry hidden locality. |
| metric proximity | physical accessibility | Locality must be independently demonstrated. |
| physical | material | Physical consequentiality can precede Matter. |
| extension | pre-existing container | Extension can emerge from non-collapsible coordinated localization. |
| geometric dimension | spatial dimension | Only modes that alter physical localization deserve the spatial interpretation. |
| effective smoothness | fundamental continuity | Smoothness may be resolution-relative. |
| detectable irregularity | fundamental resolution | A failed coarse-graining may expose only the next level. |
| responsive geometry | derived gravity | Dynamic spatial coordination merely preserves a later possibility. |
| wave-like relational transformation | quantum wavefunction | A wavefunction requires additional unearned quantum structure. |
