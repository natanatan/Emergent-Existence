# 12.26 Computational Diagnostics

Relabeling invariance. Renaming lower-order elements must not change the effective geometry.

Representation invariance. Equivalent graph, hypergraph, matrix, tensor-network, or other faithful encodings should preserve the declared geometric invariants.

Decomposition perturbation. Small arbitrary changes in descriptive partition should not radically alter the effective geometry unless they correspond to genuine changes in the organization.

Hidden-dimension control. No coordinate tuple, target dimensionality, Euclidean neighborhood, or embedding may be encoded in the generative rules if dimensionality is the quantity being tested.

Rank recovery. The number of stable independent modes should be measured from the generated organization rather than stipulated.

Probe robustness. Heterogeneous admissible probes should recover the same higher-order geometric invariants over the regime in which effective objectivity is claimed.

Scale transition. Coarse-graining may change lower-order distances, but it should preserve the declared higher-order relations when the effective geometry is claimed to survive.

Curvature diagnostic. Route-dependent failure of relational standards to return equivalently should be distinguished from artifacts introduced by coordinate representation or probe asymmetry.

Random-control comparison. Candidate multilayer systems should be compared with matched random organizations to test whether low-dimensional coherence is selected rather than ubiquitous.

Failure visibility. If no stable rank, neighborhood structure, or cross-frame invariant emerges, the result should count against the candidate mechanism rather than being redescribed as a different hidden geometry.

Scaling-dimension estimate. A claimed scaling dimension must hold across a declared window of at least one decade of scale, agree between independent estimators such as box counting and the walk-based spectral estimate, survive changes of resolution, and differ from the value obtained on matched random organizations.
