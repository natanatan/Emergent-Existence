# 4.4 The Diagnostic Transformation Family 𝔗test

Let 𝔗<sub>test</sub> denote a declared family of transformations used in the theory’s metalanguage to test preservation. A member T may alter the carrier, the realization of the poles, the relation that instantiates non-equivalence, or the embedding of the dyad, provided its source and target types are explicit.

Because 𝔗<sub>test</sub> is diagnostic, it may include collapse maps without reintroducing primitive stasis into 𝒰<sub>min</sub>. A constant structural test and an internal successor pair are different types of object. The no-stasis decision in Chapter 3 remains intact.
