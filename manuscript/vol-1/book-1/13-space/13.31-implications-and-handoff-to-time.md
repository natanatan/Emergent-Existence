# 13.31 Implications and Handoff to Time

Space has now produced something that earlier chapters could not legitimately assume: distinct, neighboring, spatially coordinated local frames.

▪ W<sub>A</sub>, W<sub>B</sub>

A relational transformation begins to alter the state recoverable at W<sub>A</sub>. Eventually, that transformation becomes consequential at W<sub>B</sub>:

▪ W<sub>A</sub> ↦ W<sub>B</sub>

Space tells us that the frames are distinct. It tells us that their separation is spatially meaningful. It tells us that their localizations belong to one coordinated domain. It may even permit their geometric organization to respond dynamically while the transformation propagates.

But Space cannot yet tell us how much propagation separates these two relational events.

▪ coordinated spatial frames ⇝ propagation between frames ⇝ comparative propagation interval ⇝ Time

Handoff to Time. Space makes relational loci mutually situated. Time must determine how relational change acquires measurable separation as it propagates among them.
