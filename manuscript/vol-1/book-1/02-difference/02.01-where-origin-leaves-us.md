# 2.1 Where Origin Leaves Us

Origin supplies the least-resolved reference condition the framework can name without treating that condition as a place, moment, substance, or cause. Its formal statement is a claim about the initial resolution available to the framework, not about an absolute metaphysical void.

▪ Res<sub>W₀</sub>(Ω) = ∅

Nothing in that expression says that reality is absolutely featureless. It says only that no operative distinction is available at the initial declared resolution. The next step must therefore be constructive without pretending that a physical event has occurred. Time has not been earned, so “next” means derivationally next.

Foundational question. What is the minimum structure required for non-equivalence to be available without already presupposing objects, properties, measurement, orientation, or succession?
