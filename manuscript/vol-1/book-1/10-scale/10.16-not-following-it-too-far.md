# 10.16 Not Following It Too Far

A striking case can become a distraction. That the Fibonacci ratio approaches φ does not show that φ is a constant of emergence. Consider the general recurrence:

▪ mag<sub>n+1</sub> = a·mag<sub>n</sub> + b·mag<sub>n-1</sub>, with characteristic relation λ² = aλ + b

Different coefficients select different dominant ratios. The safe conclusion is narrow: φ is one fixed ratio generated by one recurrence. Any stronger claim would need an independent reason why our ontology should prefer a = b = 1, and we have none.

▪ stable recursive scaling ⇏ φ

◌ SPECULATIVE Nothing here privileges φ. The Register records golden-ratio universality as Rejected, and this section is where that rejection is demonstrated rather than asserted.
