# 1.15 What Origin Has Not Earned

A physical first moment or temporal beginning.

A spatial location, metric, coordinate system, topology, or geometry.

Matter, fields, energy, action, entropy, or thermodynamic dynamics.

Objects, observers, agents, consciousness, or meaning as primitives.

Causation or a physical law of evolution.

Information in the stronger retained and consequential sense used in Boundary.

A mechanism explaining why the first distinction occurs.

A proof that Ω is an entity rather than a definitional role.

A claim that Ω contains hidden structure.

A claim that Ω is absolutely featureless.

A proof that relational ontology is physically instantiated.

Physical spacetime. Book I ends at earned Time; physically instantiated spacetime is Book II’s frontier.

The refusal to claim these results is part of the method. Origin succeeds only if it leaves the framework poorer than ordinary intuition and makes the next constructive burden unmistakable.
