# 2.8 Difference Is Not Yet a Configuration Space

The labels a and b should not yet be read as fully typed configurations. Doing so would silently introduce a state space and make transitions among its members look available before any transition relation has been admitted. Chapter 2 therefore stops one step earlier.

The poles are positions in the primitive dyadic structure. Chapter 3 will make an explicit type promotion by collecting them into the minimal configuration carrier 𝒞<sub>𝔇</sub> = {a,b}. That promotion is not free: it is part of what becomes necessary once admissible succession enters the framework.
