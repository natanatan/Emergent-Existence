# 20.26 What Would Count Against the Claim

The central claim would be substantially weakened if propagating electromagnetic behavior could not be generated from recursive relational dynamics without inserting the known optical laws directly.

It would also count against the proposal if the recursive return's angular momentum and the transverse handedness cannot be kept mathematically distinct; if phase cannot be derived from recurrent relational state; if the coherence envelope cannot reproduce diffraction and interference; if the same processes cannot account for both continuous propagation and discrete delivery; or if the model implies departures from electromagnetic and relativistic behavior where precision experiments show none.

A subtler failure would occur if every success required a separate special-purpose rule. The framework depends on generative economy: a small relational grammar should produce many observed phenomena through different organizations of the same resources.
