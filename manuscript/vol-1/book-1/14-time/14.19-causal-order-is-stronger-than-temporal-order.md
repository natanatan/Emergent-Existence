# 14.19 Causal Order Is Stronger Than Temporal Order

Boundary introduces ≺ as a dependency ordering and leaves open whether it supports causal or temporal order (4.11). “Precursor of causal order” is therefore the correct level of claim. Dependency is necessary for many forms of causation, but causation adds constraints not yet present here: locality or admissible influence structure, intervention-sensitive counterfactual relations, and—within relativistic physics—a causal geometry that separates possible influence from impossible influence.

The chapter therefore states the hierarchy with the open bridge made explicit:

▪ retained dependency ⇝ temporal ordering ⇝ spatiotemporal constraint ⇝ ? ⇝ physical causal structure

The question mark is methodological, not decorative. It marks the point at which Book II must determine whether a causal geometry emerges from the structures Book I has earned.
