# 7.1 Where Closure Leaves Us

Through Closure, we can inspect a rich relational organization without yet finding a justified direction. Closure gives us persistent higher-order identities, retained-dependency structure, and an endogenous organizational anchor A<sub>F</sub> when representative-independent addressability is available. It does not tell us that one relational role points toward another.

Now we change the question. Instead of asking whether two roles differ, we ask what happens when exchanging them changes the organization’s consequences.

If reversal matters, the relational scene has acquired a bearing. In Orientation, we ask for the weakest form of that bearing before any spatial axis, causal arrow, or metric time is allowed.

A closed organization may remain internally exchange-symmetric. The burden of this chapter is to identify the weakest condition under which exchange symmetry ceases to hold in a way that matters to the organization itself.

▪ Difference through Closure supply retained dependency, integrated organization, and endogenous reference without supplying direction. Open question: whether orientation can be derived from that inheritance alone or requires an additional commitment.
