# 9.17 Relations Among Relations

Once a relation is itself a stable, addressable structure, it can participate as a relatum in a higher-order relation, written schematically μ(r<sub>a</sub>, r<sub>b</sub>). This does not automatically create a new ontological category. The same relational grammar may be recursively re-instantiated at a higher organizational level.

▪ relation ⇝ closed relational organization ⇝ higher-order relatum ⇝ relation among higher-order relata

The line-graph building in graph theory. There, edges of one graph become vertices of another, is one encoding of the move, though the ontological claim is more general. The formal challenge is to prevent an uncontrolled type regress. If relations among relations always require a new primitive relation type, our framework proliferates categories. If higher-order relata are equivalence classes or closed organizations of lower-order structure, recursive building can proceed with the same grammar.

▪ same relational grammar, new organizational level

Open question: whether relations can become relata recursively without generating an infinite hierarchy of primitive types (Question 7).
