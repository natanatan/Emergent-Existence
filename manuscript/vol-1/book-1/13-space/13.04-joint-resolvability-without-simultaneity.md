# 13.4 Joint Resolvability Without Simultaneity

Before two loci can be spatially related, they must be jointly available within one effective relational organization. Let Joint(A,B) mean that A and B are recoverable as distinct loci within the same effective geometry Geo.

▪ Joint(A,B) ≠ A and B exist at the same time

At this stage joint resolvability means only that both loci participate in one relational configuration from which their mutual geometric organization can be resolved. A safer vocabulary is therefore joint instantiation, joint resolvability, or configurational copresence.

The ontology has earned plurality within one configuration.
