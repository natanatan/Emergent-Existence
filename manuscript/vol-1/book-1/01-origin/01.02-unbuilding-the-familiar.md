# 1.2 Unbuilding the Familiar

Look up from the page. There are things in places, some near and some far, some changing and some holding still. None of that took effort. The sorting was finished before you looked. The distinctions you make without trying, and the assumptions that let you make them, are your reference frame. This section takes it apart one layer at a time.

Start with place. To say that something is here rather than there already distinguishes positions. Near and far add magnitude to that separation. A path adds ordered connection. Geometry organizes such relations into a coherent form.

Now peel back the place layer.

Without geometric position, an object can still be conceived as something that remains itself while other things differ from it. But that already presupposes boundary and persistence. An object has to be distinguishable from what it is not, and its identity must survive at least some change.

Now peel back the objecthood layer.

What remains might still be an organization: several relations that constrain one another strongly enough to behave as a unit. Yet organization already assumes relations, and relations already assume that something can differ.

Now peel back the organization layer.

Perhaps difference can persist through transformation. If it does, the difference can constrain what follows. That is already close to information in the stronger sense the later chapters will use.

Now peel back the persistence layer.

Look up again. There is nothing left to sort, only the vicinity of difference itself. Now ask one more question:

What must be available before even a difference can become operative?

At that point there is no useful scene left to imagine. A scene would already have parts. A background would already distinguish what lies against it. A point would invite location. A moment would invite time.

The descent therefore does not uncover a hidden picture of the beginning. It removes our entitlement to picture one.

What remains is a conceptual limit: the least resolved condition from which the possibility of distinction can be discussed without first importing the world that distinction will later help to build.
