# Chapter 20 · Light: When Information Propagates Across Space

***Claim*** Light emerges when electromagnetic organization forms an *open recursive closure*: local recursive cycles that complete and reconcile an excitation state, joined to a hand-off that re-instantiates the completed state in the neighboring region, with its phase, handedness, amplitude and direction kept recoverable from one hand-off to the next.
