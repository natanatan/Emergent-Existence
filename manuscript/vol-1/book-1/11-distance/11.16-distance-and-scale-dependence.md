# 11.16 Distance and Scale Dependence

Scale gives us reason to expect that distance may depend on organizational resolution. A fine-grained description may distinguish many lower-order identities while a coarser frame treats them as one higher-order identity.

The relational chains available at the fine level need not match those available after coarse-graining, so:

d^(u)(A,B) may differ from d^(u′)(A,B), where u and u′ are the standards of two scale regimes

That does not necessarily mean one is false. They may describe separation at different organizational resolutions. The important requirement is consistency: does the coarse-grained distance preserve the relational distinctions relevant at that scale?

Distance may be scale-relative while still being structurally objective within a declared scale regime.

This is a candidate proposition, not yet a result.
