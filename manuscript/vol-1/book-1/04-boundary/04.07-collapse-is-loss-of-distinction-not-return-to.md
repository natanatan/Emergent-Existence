# 4.7 Collapse Is Loss of Distinction, Not Return to Ω

Suppose a diagnostic map T<sub>c</sub> sends both poles to one effective image q:

T<sub>c</sub>(a)=q T<sub>c</sub>(b)=q

Then Pres<sub>W</sub>(a,b;T<sub>c</sub>) fails for any frame that resolves only the target structure presented. Nothing has returned to Ω. No privileged origin was assigned. Loss is simply collapse of the previously recoverable non-equivalence.

This makes no appeal to a marked pole such as S₀ = Ω, and lets the negative case be stated symmetrically.
