# 13.7 From Geometric Position to Spatial Position

▪ reference state ⇝ relational locus ⇝ metric locus ⇝ geometric locus ⇝ spatial position

A relational locus is distinguishable by its relations. A metric locus participates in recoverable separation magnitude. A geometric locus participates in coherent higher-order organization among those separations. A spatial position participates in coordinated localization such that its geometric relations constrain the organization of the surrounding domain.

▪ spatial position = stable localization role within coordinated geometry

Coordinates may later represent this role. They do not generate it.
