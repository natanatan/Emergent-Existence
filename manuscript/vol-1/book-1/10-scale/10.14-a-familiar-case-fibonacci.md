# 10.14 A Familiar Case: Fibonacci

Only now, with magnitude, proportion and recursive comparison in hand, is the familiar case worth approaching. Suppose a composition rule yields magnitudes satisfying:

▪ mag<sub>n+1</sub> = mag<sub>n</sub> + mag<sub>n-1</sub>

With the conventional starting values this produces 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, and so on. The statement is conditional: if relational composition produces this recurrence in magnitude, Fibonacci growth follows. Nothing in the ontology says that recursion must take this form.

▪ a familiar pattern is not evidence because we recognize it
