# 3.14 Distinction Is Not Yet Information

Information requires more than an admissible difference. At minimum, some distinction must remain available so that later structural possibilities depend on it. Chapter 3 supplies an operative contrast but has not yet shown that the contrast survives any nontrivial transformation of the structure itself.

The sequence therefore remains:

▪ Difference ⇝ Distinction ⇝ retained distinction ⇝ information
