# 2.18 Implications and Handoff

Difference has earned the first non-equivalence but has earned no activity. The dyad can be described, but it cannot yet succeed itself, preserve itself, branch, or constrain a later configuration. That stopping point is intentional.

The next chapter therefore asks a sharper question than the older “what resolves the pair?” formulation: what additional commitment makes the primitive non-equivalence enter the structure’s own admissible succession without privileging either pole?
