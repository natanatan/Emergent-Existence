# 13.20 Closure Is Still Not Enclosure

Spatial realization must not erase the earlier distinction between Closure and Boundary. A system may be dependency-closed without occupying a spatially enclosed region. A region may be spatially enclosed while remaining relationally open.

▪ closure ≠ enclosure

Once Space exists, however, enclosure can become one possible realization of relational closure. This distinction will later become important wherever spatial membranes, bounded physical systems, or higher-order self-maintaining structures appear.
