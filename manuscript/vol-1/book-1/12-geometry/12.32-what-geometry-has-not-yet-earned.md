# 12.32 What Geometry Has Not Yet Earned

Geometry has not yet established that its dimensions are physical spatial dimensions. It has not established that three dimensions are uniquely selected. It has not shown that the substrate is fundamentally continuous or discrete. It has not shown that the effective geometry is globally smooth. It has not derived matter, quantum fields, particles, metric time, spacetime, gravity, energy, action, or causal speed.

It has not shown that a measured field excitation is literally a projection from a multilayer geometric substrate. It has established only a conceptual route by which interaction-conditioned projection could preserve stable multidimensional invariants while remaining ontologically incomplete.

It has not shown that curvature is gravity. It has not shown that any relational or physical domain is fractal, or that a spectral dimension falling toward two at small scales follows from the framework. It has not shown that measurement back-action is quantum collapse. It has not shown that every physical property can be reduced to geometry.

Those restrictions are strengths. They keep the chapter from converting a candidate generative mechanism into a total ontology before the necessary bridges have been built.
