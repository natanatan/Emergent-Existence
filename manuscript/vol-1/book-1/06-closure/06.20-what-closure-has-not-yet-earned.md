# 6.20 What Closure Has Not Yet Earned

Spatial enclosure. Relational inside and outside are membership in closure, not a geometric interior or exterior.

Metric distance. Closure supplies no interval, norm, separation magnitude or nearest-neighbor geometry.

Physical dimensionality. Recursive higher-order units do not yet imply spatial axes or a 3+1 regime.

Orientation. A closed organization need not have a directed internal order. Orientation carries that burden.

Metric time. Dynamic closure is preservation across ordered update, not duration in seconds or proper time.

Threshold aggregation. Cardinality-based, majority and weighted membership rules remain Deferred.

Self-consciousness. Ontic closure and even self-maintenance do not entail an internal self-model, much less phenomenal awareness.

Physical causation. Relational addressability may later support causal coarse-graining, but causal structure has not yet been derived.

Physical instantiation. The chapter has not shown why closed relational organization acquires the dynamics identified with physics.

Geometry from closure. The chapter defines closure without geometry; it does not yet show that repeated closure generates geometry.

The refusal to claim these results is part of the derivation. Closure is useful only if it adds one identifiable layer without borrowing from later ones.
