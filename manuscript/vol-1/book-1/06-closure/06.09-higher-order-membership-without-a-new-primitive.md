# 6.9 Higher-Order Membership Without a New Primitive

Relation (5.12) already showed that higher-order membership can be stated through a shared structural participation criterion without a primitive aggregation operator. Closure does not reopen that question. What it adds is a stronger source for membership: participation in the dependency structure constitutive of F.

▪ No independent aggregation primitive is required for closure membership. Cardinal and weighted membership await machinery that can justify magnitude or valuation over relations.
