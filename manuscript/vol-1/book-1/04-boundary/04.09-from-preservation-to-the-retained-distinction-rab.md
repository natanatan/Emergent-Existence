# 4.9 From Preservation to the Retained Distinction rab

When the reciprocal distinction remains recoverable under the declared relevant transformation regime, write r<sub>ab</sub> for that retained distinction. The notation does not introduce a binary digit, an orientation, or a numerical amount of information.

r<sub>ab</sub> = retained reciprocal non-equivalence of a and b

At this level r<sub>ab</sub>=r<sub>ba</sub> in content: the indices identify the participating roles but do not order them. A later oriented relation may distinguish those orderings, but Boundary does not.

“Relational bit” may remain editorial shorthand only if Appendix A states clearly that r<sub>ab</sub> is not a computational bit b∈{0,1}. “Retained distinction” is the ontologically safer term.
