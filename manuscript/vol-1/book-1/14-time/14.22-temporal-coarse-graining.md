# 14.22 Temporal Coarse-Graining

A viable emergent time should survive changes of resolution over some regime. Suppose a fine-grained chain contains many retained updates while a coarse-graining map C groups them into stable higher-order transitions:

▪ C: {\[e₀\] ≺ₜ \[e₁\] ≺ₜ … ≺ₜ \[eₙ\]} ↦ {\[E₀\] ≺ₜ \[E₁\] ≺ₜ … ≺ₜ \[Eₘ\]}, with m \< n

The exact update count changes, but the higher-order precedence relation should remain recoverable if temporal order is objective at that scale. This suggests a temporal analogue of the scale-robustness tests used for Geometry and Space.

Metric duration may renormalize under coarse-graining, while precedence remains invariant. That separation is useful because it identifies which part of Time is structurally fundamental in the candidate account and which part may be an effective quantitative representation.
