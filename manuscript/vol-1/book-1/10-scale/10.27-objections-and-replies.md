# 10.27 Objections and Replies

**Objection 1:** Bijection already assumes sets. **Reply:** Set notation belongs to the metalanguage. What the model requires is one-to-one correspondence among distinguishable members, which Distinction supplies. If the correspondence itself proves unavailable, the chapter fails.

---

**Objection 2:** The numeral labels make the detour pointless. **Reply:** For calculation, yes. For derivation, no. The chapter tests whether what a numeral compresses can be produced without assuming the numeral, and it can.

---

**Objection 3:** Cardinality is too crude to ground physical scale. **Reply:** Agreed, and it is not asked to. It is the weakest magnitude available here, and its job is to cross from no magnitude to some.

---

**Objection 4:** Infinite cardinality behaves unlike finite size. **Reply:** Correct. The foundational use here is finite. Infinite cases are noted and not relied upon.

---

**Objection 5:** Ratio arithmetic has still been imported. **Reply:** Ratio appears only after finite cardinal comparison, and 10.10 says plainly that the structural statement of proportion is unfinished without it. That is a declared debt, not a concealed one.

---

**Objection 6:** Fibonacci was chosen to manufacture φ. **Reply:** It is treated as a conditional worked case, and 10.16 shows the general recurrence selecting other ratios. Any claim privileging φ would need independent motivation the framework does not have.

---

**Objection 7:** A stable ratio is only the analyst’s calculation. **Reply:** It becomes ontologically relevant only if the modelled organization realizes the comparative relation and the stability survives the diagnostics of 10.25. Otherwise it is arithmetic about a model.

---

**Objection 8:** A reusable standard is already a unit. **Reply:** Not yet. It is a repeatable comparative relation. A unit needs a calibration scheme, which later chapters must earn.

---

**Objection 9:** Coarse-graining is circular, since it presupposes smallness. **Reply:** That is why the operator appears only after comparative magnitude, and why it is indexed to an earned standard rather than to a metric.

---

**Objection 10:** Self-similarity is already fractal and therefore geometric. **Reply:** Self-similarity is stated here as preservation of organization under a scale transformation. The fractal reading needs a metric and an embedding, and both are withheld.

---

**Objection 11:** Multiple scale factors form a vector. **Reply:** A family of scale relations is not a vector space. Addition, scalar multiplication, basis and inner product all remain unearned, as 9.19 requires.

---

**Objection 12:** Magnitude depends on how an organization is decomposed. **Reply:** This is the chapter’s most serious vulnerability, and it is not fully answered. The membership and decomposition criteria must be constrained independently, as F must be in Closure and Return, or magnitude becomes an artifact of description. The Register carries it as open.
