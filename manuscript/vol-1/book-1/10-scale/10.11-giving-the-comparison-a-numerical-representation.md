# 10.11 Giving the Comparison a Numerical Representation

Correspondence has given us proportion, and it is laborious. Exhibiting a bijection between W × B and A × A is exact and unwieldy, which is what notation is for. With finite cardinal magnitude available, let mag(X) be the earned magnitude of X. The same relation compresses to a ratio:

▪ Cmp(X, Y) ↦ mag(X)/mag(Y)

and the part-to-whole pattern of 10.10 becomes statable:

▪ mag(W)/mag(A) = mag(A)/mag(B)

The order matters and is the point. The ratio represents a comparative relation that 10.10 established by correspondence. It does not create that relation by being written. If the notation were withdrawn the relation would still hold. Read backwards, this looks like ordinary arithmetic about sizes. Read forwards, it is a compression of pairings we could have carried out one at a time.

▪ Ratio is a compression of the correspondence of 10.10, not an additional primitive. Open question: whether any later result needs the ratio form rather than the correspondence behind it.
