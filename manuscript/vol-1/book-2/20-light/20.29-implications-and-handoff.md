# 20.29 Implications and Handoff

We can now describe light as electromagnetic organization maintaining its identity through open recursive propagation:

$$\text{light} = \text{self-maintaining open recursive electromagnetic closure}$$

Its local cycles complete and reconcile the excitation state in place. Its completed state is handed into neighboring relations at a rate set by the medium. Its envelope recruits surrounding relations into graded participation. Its direction is stabilized by inherited orientation and neighborhood feedback. Its wavelength, frequency, amplitude, polarization and momentum emerge from the geometry and energy of that propagating organization, and its energy is delivered in units only where an interaction closes it.

The remaining transition is profound. Light preserves identity by continuing through space. Matter will require energetic organization to preserve identity through local persistence:

$$\text{open propagating closure} + \text{relational capture} \rightarrow \text{candidate persistent local closure}$$

Relational capture occurs when interaction changes the admissible geometry strongly enough that energy previously committed to open propagation becomes recursively self-retaining within a localized domain. Whether the opposed internal charge states of 20.17 take part in that capture is open (Question 15).

The distinction gives us the next question:

How can energy that maintains itself by moving become energy that maintains itself by staying?

That question carries us into matter.
