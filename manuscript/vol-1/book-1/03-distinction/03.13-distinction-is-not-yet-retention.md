# 3.13 Distinction Is Not Yet Retention

A distinction may be operative in succession and still fail to survive a transformation of the larger structure that supports it. Under 𝒰<sub>min</sub>, reciprocal exchange preserves the dyad by construction; testing only J<sub>𝒰</sub> would therefore make retention trivial.

Boundary must ask a stronger question: what happens when the update structure is perturbed, embedded, projected, or otherwise compared across a declared transformation family? That problem is deliberately deferred.
