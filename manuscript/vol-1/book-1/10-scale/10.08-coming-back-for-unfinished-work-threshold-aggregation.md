# 10.8 Coming Back for Unfinished Work: Threshold Aggregation

Relation left something undone. Existential and universal aggregation reduced to quantification (5.12). However, threshold aggregation, most of them, at least three, a majority, needed cardinality and was deferred to this chapter. We can come back for it now.

Let A<sub>P</sub> be the relations satisfying a predicate P:

▪ A<sub>P</sub> = { r ∈ ℜ : P(r) }

Before any numeral, let K be a standard finite collection. The threshold condition is relationally expressible as an injection:

▪ K ↪ A<sub>P</sub>

Once cardinal labels exist, the same condition compresses to mag(A<sub>P</sub>) ≥ k. Nothing was added to our ontology beyond comparative magnitude. A debt from Relation is paid with an instrument earned here.

▪ Threshold aggregation needs no primitive beyond correspondence, which closes the Relation deferral. Open question: whether weighted aggregation, which needs more than cardinality, can be earned at all in this volume.
