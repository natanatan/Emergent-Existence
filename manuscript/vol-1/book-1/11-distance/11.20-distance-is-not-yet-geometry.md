# 11.20 Distance Is Not Yet Geometry

The existence of a metric does not automatically give us a familiar space. We still lack dimension, coordinates, angles, orthogonality, curvature, continuity, neighborhood topology as ontology, manifold structure, and spatial embedding.

A finite metric structure may be highly irregular. It may not resemble any low-dimensional Euclidean or Lorentzian geometry.

▪ metric separation ≠ geometry

Distance earns a precursor, not the destination. Geometry will ask when a coherent relational metric structure can be represented as an effective geometry with stable dimensions and coordinate-like degrees of freedom.
