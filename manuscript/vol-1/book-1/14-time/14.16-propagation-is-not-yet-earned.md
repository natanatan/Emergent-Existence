# 14.16 Propagation Is Not Yet Earned

It is tempting to interpret an ordered transition from a state localized at one spatial region to a later state localized elsewhere as propagation. That interpretation is premature. Propagation requires a relation between spatial separation and temporal order. Space earned spatial localization; this chapter is constructing temporal order. Their coupling has not yet been derived.

Accordingly, the present chapter can describe changes whose retained consequences appear in distinct local relational states, but it must not assign a propagation speed:

▪ speed = spatial separation / temporal duration

because the numerator and denominator have not yet been shown to participate in one invariant physical structure. The equation is familiar physics, not a premise available to the derivation.

This boundary becomes the central handoff at the end of Book I.
