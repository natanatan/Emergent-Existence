# 13.19 Boundary Can Now Become Spatial

Boundary was introduced earlier as preserved non-equivalence, not as a wall or surface. Only now can that earlier distinction acquire literal spatial realization. Suppose a domain D contains localization classes whose relations distinguish membership in D from non-membership. Once those classes are spatially coordinated, a relational membership boundary may be represented geometrically as partial D.

▪ relational boundary ⇝ geometric boundary ⇝ spatial boundary

Only at this stage do familiar terms such as inside, outside, region, enclosure, and surface acquire literal spatial interpretation.
