# 20.20 Momentum Without Rest Mass

How can light deliver a push while having no rest mass?

An organization with mass keeps its identity through a recurrence localized in a bounded region, and in its own rest frame that recurrence continues while the organization stays put (Chapter 21). A luminous excitation keeps its identity only by being handed forward. It has no rest frame, because the organization that constitutes it is propagation.

Momentum follows from the direction of that propagation. The measured relation (20.3), which Chapter 18's invariant rate should supply for any excitation with no rest-frame cycle, is

$$E = pc\quad\quad\text{or}\quad\quad p = \frac{E}{c}$$

where E is the energy of the excitation (joules, J) and p its momentum (kilogram-metres per second, kg·m/s). When the excitation's directed state is absorbed or scattered, part or all of that momentum is transferred, which is radiation pressure. Light delivers its whole energy divided by c as forward push. An organization with mass delivers less forward push than its energy divided by c, because part of its energy stays in its internal cycle. So whatever the update cycle carries in light, it does not take energy away from forward momentum.
