# 10.23 Several Modes, Several Scales

Relation counted independent relational degrees of variation and refused to call the count a rank. Orientation added orientational modes and left their relation open. Nothing requires one scale relation to govern all of them. Different modes may stabilize at different comparative ratios, or some may stabilize while others do not.

▪ one organization may carry several scale relations at once

Open question: whether a family of scale relations over independent modes composes into anything stronger, which would need structure Formalism declined to import (Question 11).
