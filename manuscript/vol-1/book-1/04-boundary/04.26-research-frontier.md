# 4.26 Research Frontier

Transformation selection. What later physical principle selects the transformation family under which a distinction should count as robust or retained?

Frame realization. When does an abstract resolving frame W become a physically instantiated frame rather than a criterion in the metalanguage?

Partial retention. Can recoverability be graded without importing probability, metric distance, or information measure too early?

Dependency structure. Under what additional conditions does ≺ become transitive, acyclic, or sufficiently stable to support temporal interpretation?

Identity. What conditions make ~<sub>F</sub> more than a classification rule and yield autonomous higher-order organization?

Anchor emergence. Can Closure earn a stable endogenous reference role without contradicting the symmetric inheritance established here?

Information. Can familiar quantitative information measures be recovered as coarse-grained or specialized cases of consequential retained distinction?
