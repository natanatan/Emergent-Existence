# 4.18 Boundary Is Not Yet an Anchor

Neither W, F, B<sub>ab</sub> nor r<sub>ab</sub> selects one pole as a privileged reference. A resolving frame discriminates; an identity criterion classifies; a boundary preserves non-collapse. None of those operations by itself produces a center or origin.

The current candidate is that Closure may later earn endogenous reference: a sufficiently closed organization can become a stable reference condition for subsequent organization. That proposal reverses the old assumption that the anchor was inherited from S₀=Ω and must be tested explicitly when Closure is revised.
