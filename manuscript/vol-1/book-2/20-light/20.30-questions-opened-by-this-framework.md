# Questions Opened by This Framework

1.  What explicit update and hand-off rules are sufficient to generate stable open recursive closure?

2.  How does recurrent relational state generate phase as a measurable variable?

3.  Can the recursive return's angular momentum and the transverse handedness be formally distinguished without redundancy?

4.  Can the orthogonality and transverse organization earned in Chapter 19 be preserved dynamically through propagation rather than imposed?

5.  What measurable consequence does the recursive return's angular momentum have, given that the rotational push delivered per absorbed unit is ±ħ at every frequency?

6.  Why is the handedness transferred per closing interaction fixed, so that E = hf follows?

7.  Does the hand-off rule keep the speed independent of frequency down to the neighborhood scale?

8.  What sets the time each neighborhood needs to resolve an incoming state, and does it vary with the density of the medium's activity?

9.  Can momentum p = E/c be shown to carry the whole energy forward with nothing withheld by the update cycle?

10. Does the signed compatibility rule reproduce classical fringes, and what does it imply for joint counts in two-detector experiments?

11. Can the coherence-envelope model reproduce single-slit and double-slit diffraction quantitatively?

12. What relational variables decide among reflection, refraction, scattering, transmission and absorption at a boundary?

13. What determines the directional pattern of emission from a source's geometry?

14. What degree of transformation preserves luminous identity, and what threshold makes a new excitation?

15. What geometrical conditions allow interacting light to be captured into persistent organization with mass, and do the opposed internal charge states of 20.17 take part?
