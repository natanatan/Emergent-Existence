# 4.12 Boundary as a Preserved Criterion of Non-Collapse

Boundary enters when preservation is treated as a criterion rather than a single successful case. A primitive Boundary is the relational condition under which a distinction remains recoverable across the transformation regime relevant to the identity under consideration.

For a declared frame W and diagnostic family 𝔗\*, define the preservation class:

▪ B<sub>ab</sub>(W,𝔗\*) = {T ∈ 𝔗\* : Pres<sub>W</sub>(a,b;T)}

This set is bookkeeping for the boundary condition, not a spatial region. It separates preserving transformations from collapsing transformations. There is still no inside, outside, surface, distance, or enclosure.
