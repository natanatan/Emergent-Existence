# 20.15 Where Planck's Constant Comes From

The onset of quantum behavior was placed at the de Broglie wavelength, λ = h/p. That relation contains Planck's constant, and the framework has not yet said where Planck's constant comes from.

There are two possibilities. Either *h* is derived, emerging as the natural size of a cycle in the relational field (the quantity that, in standard physics, relates a cycle's frequency to its energy, E = hf), or it is taken as an input, a measured constant the framework accepts as given. Chapter 10 (Scale) earned magnitude measured by an organization's own standards, and the most promising route to deriving *h* runs through it: if the cycles earned in Return have a smallest stable size, that size would act as a quantum of action. Nothing in Book I or Book II so far fixes that size, and the chapter does not claim to.

The difference matters. If *h* is an input, the de Broglie onset is borrowed from physics and interpreted by the framework. If *h* is derived, the onset becomes a prediction. A Planck-scale threshold would need two further constants, the invariant speed *c* and the gravitational coupling *G*, so any claim about the Planck length also depends on what Chapters 18 and 19 have earned.
