# 20.6 Mutual Reinforcement

A propagating closure holds several properties whose relations must remain mutually supportive.

The measured form of that support is induction. A changing magnetic field drives an electric field (Faraday 1831), and a changing electric field drives a magnetic one (Maxwell 1865, confirmed by Hertz 1887). Each part's change sustains the other, which is how the pair can keep going without anything pushing it from behind. Chapter 19 earns this coupling; light is what the coupling does when it is free to travel.

The properties involved, with the unit of each, are:

-   φ, **phase**: where the excitation stands within its update cycle (radians, rad);

-   $L_{\text{closure}}$, the **angular momentum of the recursive return** that establishes the open closure and maintains its relational states (joule-seconds, J·s; ◌);

-   χ, **handedness and transverse orientation**: the orientation of the electric and magnetic parts in the plane across the travel, including its sense of rotation; this is what a polarizing filter registers (rad);

-   A, **amplitude**: the strength of organized participation (for the electric part, volts per metre, V/m);

-   $\widehat{k}$, **direction of travel** (a unit direction, no unit);

-   N, the relevant **state of the neighboring region**.

The requirement for continued propagation can then be written as

$$\mathcal{C}\left( \varphi, L_{\text{closure}},\chi, A,\widehat{k}, N \right) \geq \mathcal{C}_{\min}$$

where $\mathcal{C}$ is the compatibility of the complete propagating organization with its successor state and $\mathcal{C}_{\min}$ is the minimum needed for the open closure to continue.

The notation does not yet provide a dynamical law. It names the structural burden: an excitation continues only where the variables that constitute it support a compatible successor state. Persistence does not require every observable property to stay unchanged; it requires the identity-bearing relations among them to remain recoverable.

*Here, coherent means that an excitation's own phase relations stay recoverable from one hand-off to the next. It does not mean laser-like order among many excitations. Thermal light from a warm body is a crowd of excitations with unrelated phases, yet each of them is coherent in this sense over its own length, which is why thermal light propagates as well as laser light does. Every body above absolute zero radiates, so open closure is not rare. What is selective is which organizations persist: many local disturbances never close at all, and the ones that propagate are those whose update cycle and hand-off stay jointly compatible.*
