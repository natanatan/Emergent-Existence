# 4.17 Retained Distinction as Minimal Information

A retained distinction becomes informational in the weakest functional sense when its presence changes admissible possibilities. The relevant condition is not merely that r<sub>ab</sub> exists, but that retaining or removing it changes 𝒫 for some downstream configuration or domain.

▪ 𝒫(Σ,r<sub>ab</sub>) ≠ 𝒫(Σ,∅)

This is conceptual information before probability, coding, entropy, or energetic cost. It captures the minimum idea that a preserved difference can make a structural difference to what follows. Later information theories must either recover this case as a limit/precursor or show that the proposal is too weak to support the intended bridge.
