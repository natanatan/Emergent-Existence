# 13.25 The Spatial Interpretation Is Conditional

Geometry becomes Space in this framework only when geometric localization does work that a merely mathematical geometry need not do. Localization relations must constrain admissible couplings, remain recoverable when coordinate labels are removed, and survive enough variation of probe and scale to count as organization rather than as one chosen representation.

This is an interpretive threshold, not a new primitive. The inherited geometry supplies loci, metric relations, neighborhoods, and effective dimensional organization. Space is the stronger reading licensed when those structures become mutually consequential for what can be instantiated and coupled within the domain.

▪ geometric coherence + consequential coordinated localization ⇝ candidate spatial regime

The threshold remains provisional until generative models show that the coupling rule does not already contain the locality it is later said to recover. Section 13.26 therefore treats coordinate removal, locality prediction, heterogeneous probing, scale robustness, and responsive geometry as diagnostics rather than as premises.
