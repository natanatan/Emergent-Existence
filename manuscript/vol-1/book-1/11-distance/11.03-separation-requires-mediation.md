# 11.3 Separation Requires Mediation

Suppose A and B belong to the same relational organization. Their separation can be direct:

A R B

or mediated:

A R X₁ R X₂ ··· R B

The symbols describe a chain of relational dependence or incidence. One distinction must be made before the chain does any work. Formalism typed 𝒰 as the admissible transition between configurations. Mediation is not that relation. 𝒰 carries one configuration to a successor; mediation holds between loci within a single configuration. Conflating them would make distance a count of updates, which would both misdescribe separation and leave Time nothing of its own to earn.

▪ mediation between loci ≠ transition between configurations

Let an admissible relational chain from A to B be:

▪ κ<sub>AB</sub> = (A, X₁, …, Xₙ, B)

such that each neighboring pair participates in a relation admitted by the organization under the declared criterion.

Some pairs may be connected through fewer mediating relations than others. “Fewer” is now legitimate only because Scale earned comparative finite magnitude. Before Scale, counting intermediates would have been external bookkeeping. After Scale, the organization may itself support comparison among finite chains.

▪ relational mediation + earned magnitude ⇝ comparable separation
