# 4.2 Persistence Is Not Stasis

With no self-loop in 𝒰<sub>min</sub>, no primitive configuration persists by remaining unchanged. Persistence at this stage can only mean persistence of a structural distinction. This aligns with Proposition 1A: what matters is distinguishability surviving transformation, not a token remaining frozen through duration.

The chapter therefore avoids saying that a or b persists. A realization may be replaced completely while a distinction it instantiates remains recoverable.

▪ state invariance ≠ retained distinction
