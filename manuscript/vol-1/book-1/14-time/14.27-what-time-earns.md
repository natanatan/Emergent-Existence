# 14.27 What Time Earns

If the candidate derivation succeeds, Time earns the following structures:

inherited retained-dependency precedence ≺ as the pre-temporal substrate for ordering;

a derived strict partial-order candidate ≺ₜ on dependency-cycle equivalence classes, without a universal external clock;

ordered chains, branches, merges, and dependency-incomparable events;

a distinction between succession and mere manuscript or algorithmic indexing;

a structural record asymmetry by which some dependency orientations can be internally recovered;

a candidate route from recurrent relational processes to endogenous temporal standards;

a candidate route from cross-clock calibration to metric temporal comparison;

local temporal organization compatible with, but not yet equivalent to, later relativistic time.

The strongest earned claim is therefore not that “time flows.” It is that retained dependency can, under explicit additional conditions, be reduced to an internally recoverable strict order of succession without assuming a universal clock, and that sufficiently stable recurrent structures can in principle furnish quantitative standards for comparing that succession.
