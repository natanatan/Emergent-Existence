# 4.20 Boundary Is Not Yet Physical Time

𝒰 supplies admissible succession, and ≺ supplies retained-dependency precedence. Neither is yet elapsed duration. The formal sequence of applications can be indexed or counted in the analyst’s mathematics without thereby yielding seconds, rates, simultaneity, proper time, or an invariant causal cone.

Time will later ask whether stable dependency precedence can support temporal order and whether recurrent, countable, stable, couplable subsystems can become endogenous clocks. Boundary supplies prerequisites, not the temporal interpretation.
