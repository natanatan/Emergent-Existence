# 9.24 Formal Carrier, Not a Hidden Medium

Earlier manuscript stages used the word substrate for the total evolving relational structure. No additional medium is presently required beneath the relations. The formal carrier is the structured collection of relational configurations and admissible transitions themselves.

▪ formal carrier = relational organization under consideration

▪ formal carrier ≠ material medium beneath relation

If later physics requires a further ontological carrier, we must say so explicitly. Until then, adding a medium would duplicate explanatory work without necessity. This resolves the word conservatively: if substrate is retained, it names the total relational structure at a given level, not a substance in which relations float.
