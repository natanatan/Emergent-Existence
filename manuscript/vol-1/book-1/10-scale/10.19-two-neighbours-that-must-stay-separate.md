# 10.19 Two Neighbours That Must Stay Separate

With this much equipment, several ideas begin to look alike. Two separations matter.

Hierarchy is not scale. A quotient can sit at a higher organizational level while compressing many lower-order configurations into fewer effective ones. Higher means organizationally later, not greater in magnitude.

▪ higher-order ≠ larger

Resolution is not scale. A resolving frame fixes which distinctions are accessible; a scale relation compares magnitude. Physical theories routinely connect the two, and that connection is additional structure rather than a definition.

▪ resolution ≠ scale
