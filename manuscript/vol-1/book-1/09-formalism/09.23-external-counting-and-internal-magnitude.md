# 9.23 External Counting and Internal Magnitude

Formalism must distinguish what the analyst may count from what our ontology can internally compare. The meta-theory may freely say a simulation contains a thousand nodes or that a recurrence was iterated five hundred times.

▪ external cardinal bookkeeping ≠ endogenous magnitude

In Scale, we ask when comparison itself becomes structurally available as scale. Formalism permits finite sets, indices and model sizes in the mathematics while refusing to read them as intrinsic measures available to the modeled system. This preserves the earlier distinction between quantification and counting, and between counting and measurement.
