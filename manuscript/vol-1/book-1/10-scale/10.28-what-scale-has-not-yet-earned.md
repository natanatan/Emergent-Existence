# 10.28 What Scale Has Not Yet Earned

Metric distance or length.

A physical unit: a metre, a second, a Planck length, an energy scale.

A coordinate system.

Vector-space structure, inner products, angles, basis vectors or orthogonality.

A manifold, continuum or metric topology.

Physical dimensionality or an embedding dimension.

Fractal dimension as a property of physical space.

A universal law of self-similarity, or any universal role for Fibonacci or φ.

Renormalization-group flow as physical dynamics.

A probability measure or a stochastic distribution over scales.

What it earns is narrower: comparison of amount, proportion between amounts, and a standard the structure supplies itself.
