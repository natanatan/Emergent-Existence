# 12.11 Local Metric Coupling

A stable n-parameter description is still not a full local geometry. The modes must also stand in reproducible comparative relations. A mature differential-geometric representation might write ds^2 = g<sub>ij</sub>(x) dx^i dx^j. We should not treat that formula as foundational here, but it tells us what additional content must ultimately be explained.

The coefficients g<sub>ij</sub> specify how changes in different local modes contribute jointly to measured separation. From the present relational perspective, they can be interpreted as an effective rule summarizing local coupling among independently resolved modes.

▪ g<sub>ij</sub> ~ effective local relation among variation modes and separation

This interpretation does not claim that every metric tensor is literally generated by multilayer coupling. It identifies a possible ontological role for the metric components if the candidate substrate succeeds.

The direction of explanation remains important: local metric relations should summarize the stable organization that has emerged, rather than being inserted as unexplained numerical fields whose structure the chapter is supposed to derive.
