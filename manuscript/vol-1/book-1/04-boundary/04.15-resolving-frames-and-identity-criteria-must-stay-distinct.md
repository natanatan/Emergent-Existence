# 4.15 Resolving Frames and Identity Criteria Must Stay Distinct

W and F answer different questions. W asks what distinctions a frame can resolve. F asks which features must be preserved for two configurations to count as realizations of the same higher-order identity.

~<sub>W</sub> = frame equivalence ~<sub>F</sub> = identity equivalence

Two configurations may be indistinguishable to a coarse resolving frame yet fail the identity criterion, or be distinguishable in many details while still satisfying the same F. Conflating the relations would make observation constitutive of identity, which the framework does not assume.
