# 9.20 Simplicial Structure as One Realization, Not the Definition of Closure

Earlier versions of the project treated simplex formation as too close to our definition of closure. Closure corrected that: closure is relational dependency sufficiency, not geometric completion. Simplicial mathematics can nevertheless be recovered as one possible realization of nested incidence. There, a k-simplex has faces of lower dimension. The boundary operator maps k-chains to (k−1)-chains, with the characteristic identity ∂(∂X) = 0.

The identity matters because internal interfaces cancel under successive boundary operations. This is a useful analogy for how lower-order constitutive detail disappears from a higher-order boundary description. The implication runs one way only.

▪ earned closure ⇝ simplicial realization may be useful

▪ ∂² = 0 ⇏ foundational closure ontology
