# 7.19 What Orientation Has Not Yet Earned

Spatial direction or coordinates. Orientation remains relational and anchor-relative.

Metric distance or scale. No interval, ruler, ratio of lengths or physical size has been derived.

Metric time. Sequence and return remain generative and dependency notions.

Thermodynamic irreversibility or entropy production.

Physical causation or relativistic light cones.

Global partial order. Directed cycles remain admissible.

Geometry or dimensionality from orientation alone.

Chirality or parity violation, though oriented loops may later supply prerequisites.

A universal law that return compatibility always selects stable orientations.

A specified mechanism for the unresolved variation beneath the minimally resolved origin.
