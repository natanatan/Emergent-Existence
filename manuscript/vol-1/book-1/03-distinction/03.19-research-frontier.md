# 3.19 Research Frontier

| **Open module** | **Question to be earned** |
| --- | --- |
| Origin of 𝒰 | Can admissible transformation itself be derived from a still weaker structural principle? |
| Admissibility semantics | What physical or mathematical fact eventually distinguishes actually admissible transitions from merely conceivable ones? |
| Generalization | How should 𝒰 be extended when more than two configurations become available without presupposing Relation? |
| Branching | When does more than one admissible successor become necessary, and what new commitment does branching represent? |
| Temporal interpretation | Under what conditions does compositional succession become physical temporal order? |
| Operational realization | What candidate physical substrate could instantiate 𝒰 without assuming spacetime in advance? |
