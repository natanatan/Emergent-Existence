# 5.17 What Relation Has Not Yet Earned

The discipline of the chapter lies as much in what it refuses to add as in what it adds. Several concepts are near enough to be tempting and still too expensive.

Spatial direction. Different relational positions do not yet define left, right, inward, outward or any directed axis.

Linear order. Two positions can differ without admitting a unique ordering p<sub>i</sub> \< p<sub>j</sub>.

Metric distance. No quantity yet measures how far apart two relational positions are.

Orthogonality. Independent relational modes do not yet possess an inner product, and orthogonality must not be assumed as a capacity of the domain.

Physical dimensionality. Relational degrees of freedom are not spatial dimensions.

Metric time. Admissible succession and retained-dependency precedence are not seconds, durations or proper time.

Physical instantiation. The chapter has not shown why stable relational organization acquires the dynamical properties associated with physics.

These deferrals are not omissions to be hidden. They are the boundary conditions that make later claims testable. our framework is stronger if the reader can point to the exact chapter where each additional structure becomes necessary.
