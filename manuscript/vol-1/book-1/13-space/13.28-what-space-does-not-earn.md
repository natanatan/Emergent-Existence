# 13.28 What Space Does Not Earn

Space still does not establish metric duration, propagation interval, velocity, causal speed, temporal simultaneity, proper time, Lorentzian signature, spacetime, cosmological expansion as an actual physical law, gravity, gravitational field equations, matter, particles, quantum fields, wavefunctions, Hilbert space, superposition, Born statistics, energy, or action.

Nor does it establish that geometry is fundamentally continuous or fundamentally discrete. The chapter permits either possibility to remain testable.
