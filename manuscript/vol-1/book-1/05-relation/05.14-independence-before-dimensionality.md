# 5.14 Independence Before Dimensionality

Once a domain contains several relations, it is tempting to count independent variations and call the result dimensionality. That would be premature twice over: cardinal magnitude has not yet been earned internally, and independence need not yet have a vector-space form. Relation can nevertheless ask a weaker structural question: which admissible variations are fixed by the organization of others, and which remain non-redundant under the current constraints?

relation count ≠ independence structure ≠ physical dimension

The present chapter therefore earns an independence problem, not a numerical relational dimension d<sub>R</sub>. A set of variations is structurally non-redundant when no member is fixed by the admissible constraints imposed by the others, under a definition Formalism must make precise. Rank remains only an analogy until appropriate algebra is earned. Matroid theory (Whitney, 1935), which axiomatizes independence without presupposing a vector space, is a natural candidate formalism for later development.

A regulatory network or game-playing agent can contain several non-redundant relational variables without those variables being spatial axes. The later physical question is stronger: do generic recursive relational dynamics select stable collective modes that decouple sufficiently to support effective dimensional geometry? In particular, no orthogonality is assumed. If effective orthogonality appears, it must be earned from stability-selected decoupling or a comparably justified relation among modes.

Independence of relational variation must also remain distinct from the dimensionality of any later embedding space. A system could possess many non-redundant relational modes without each mode becoming a spatial axis. Relation therefore withholds comparisons such as d<sub>R</sub> ≤ d<sub>P</sub> or d<sub>R</sub> \> d<sub>P</sub> until both quantities and their relevant notion of magnitude have actually been earned.

Open question: how should relational independence be defined before vector-space structure is available, and do stable relational dynamics generate persistent approximately decoupled modes without dimensionality being written into the update rules?
