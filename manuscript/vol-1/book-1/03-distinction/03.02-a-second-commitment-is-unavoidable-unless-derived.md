# 3.2 A Second Commitment Is Unavoidable Unless Derived

A static relation does not by itself generate succession. If the book wants later chapters to speak of updates, histories, retention, branching, closure, or return, something must constrain what configurations may follow what. It is easy to use 𝒰 without recognizing it as a foundational cost. This chapter makes that cost explicit.

The commitment is not that physical time already flows. It is only that there exists an admissibility relation over configurations. Removing that relation leaves Difference intact but eliminates every downstream concept that depends on transformation.

▪ The framework should count 𝒰 as a primitive commitment unless an earlier or deeper construction later derives it.

Whether Difference and admissible transformation can ultimately be reduced to a single weaker principle remains a research question.
