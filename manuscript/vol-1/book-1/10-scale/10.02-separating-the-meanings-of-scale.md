# 10.2 Separating the Meanings of Scale

The word scale names several things in ordinary mathematics and physics. If we do not separate them now we can appear to advance while standing still.

| **Sense** | **What it means** | **Status here** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| External model scale | The analyst chooses a size, an iteration count, a plot range | Bookkeeping only, as 9.23 allows |
| Organizational level | One closed organization becomes a relatum at a higher level | Already available, and not magnitude |
| Resolution | Which distinctions a frame can resolve | Available since 4.3, and not magnitude |
| Comparative magnitude | One organization is the same as, larger than or proportionate to another | What this chapter earns |
| Metric scale | A ratio of lengths against a unit | Not earned here, and not in this volume until Distance |

Two separations matter immediately. A higher organizational level is not automatically larger, and a larger organization is not automatically farther from anything.

▪ organizational level ≠ magnitude

▪ scale ≠ distance

Distance stays closed on the map until Distance.
