# 10.20 Coarse-Graining, Finally

Earlier chapters discussed coarse resolution and deliberately withheld a scale-indexed coarse-graining operator; Orientation deferred it here by name. We can approach it now without circularity, provided the order is explicit:

▪ relative magnitude ⇝ comparison standard ⇝ scale-relative equivalence ⇝ coarse-graining

Let ≈<sub>s</sub> group configurations whose differences fall within a declared comparative standard s. A coarse-graining map is then written:

▪ CG<sub>s</sub> : 𝒞 ↦ 𝒞 / ≈s

The load-bearing phrase is “within a comparative standard”. This chapter supports it only where the standard has been earned relationally. Smallness by metric distance remains unavailable. This is the first legitimate scale-aware reduction of detail in our framework, and it is still pre-geometric.
