# 9.7 Composition and Identity

Relations compose when the output side of one matches the input side of another. This ordinary relation theory defines by quantifying over an intermediate state.

▪ Σ (𝒰 ∘ 𝒰) Σ″ ⟺ there exists Σ′ such that Σ 𝒰 Σ′ and Σ′ 𝒰 Σ″

This gives earlier talk of repeated application a precise meaning without requiring 𝒰 to be functional. 𝒰² Is 𝒰 ∘ 𝒰, and 𝒰^k is k compositions. An identity relation is also available.

▪ Id\_𝒞 = { (Σ, Σ) : Σ ∈ 𝒞 }

That such a relation exists does not mean nothing changes whenever identity holds. Return already separated exact state identity from higher-order equivalence. A nontrivial transition may preserve F without being the identity relation.

▪ T ≠ Id\_𝒞 while T(Σ) ~<sub>F</sub> Σ
