# 3.4 Admissibility Must Not Be Defined Through Distinction

The word “admissible” is the chapter’s most dangerous word. If a transition is called admissible because some resolver can already distinguish it, Distinction has been presupposed in the definition that is supposed to earn it. The chapter therefore refuses any definition of 𝒰 in terms of Res<sub>F</sub>, observation, measurement, or represented difference.

At this level, admissibility is a primitive structural constraint on succession. Its content is restricted by what Difference already supplies, and no hidden label may determine which transition is allowed.
