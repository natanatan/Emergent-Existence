# 10.3 The Shortcut That Fails

Faced with a map that has no scale bar, the obvious response is to write numbers beside the landmarks. That is exactly what we cannot do yet. Writing three here and five there, before explaining what makes three and five available, would import counting as a primitive and then discover it as a result.

▪ primitive numeral ⇝ magnitude — not admitted as the foundational route

We can recognize individual marks. We cannot yet assume a counting stick. What we need is a weaker operation, one that reveals sameness of magnitude without first naming the magnitude.

▪ correspondence ⇝ comparative magnitude ⇝ numeral as a later label
