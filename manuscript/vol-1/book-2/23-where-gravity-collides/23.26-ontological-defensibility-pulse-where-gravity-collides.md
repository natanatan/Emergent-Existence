# 23.26 Ontological Defensibility Pulse: Where Gravity Collides

**Entities required.** The relational field Φ (Chapter 16), detector organization, local excitation, retained distinctions, conditioned neighborhoods (Chapter 22), resolving frames, a conditioning relation between excitation and field, and a consistency condition across the two ends of one interaction. No primitive particle, pre-existing path, or collapse rule.

**Ontological level.** A change in organizational regime within relational structure already earned. "Quantum" is a correspondence label for a class of measured behavior until quantum formal structure is derived.

**Lower-order dependencies.** Distinction and transformation (Chapter 3), retention (Chapter 4), return (Chapter 8), scale (Chapter 10), distance, geometry and space (Chapters 11–13), relational fields (Chapter 16), effective geometry and causal order (Chapter 18), and local relational conditioning (Chapter 22).

**Emergent causal property.** A local excitation can reshape the field around it strongly enough to amplify further coupling toward one retained outcome, where alternatives would otherwise condition the field into incompatible neighborhoods.

**Claim type.** A relational and dynamical claim with a physical, testable threshold. The unification with gravity is ◌ Speculative.

**New primitive test.** The incompatibility measure, the excitation threshold and the feedback law must be built from earned relational quantities, or declared as new quantities with independent motivation. None may be defined by the outcome it explains.

**Parsimony test.** If the mechanism finally needs particle trajectories, a collapse rule, the Born rule, or a gravitational coupling inserted independently, it has not reduced what it set out to reduce.

**Experimental discrimination.** For each empirical claim: what was controlled, what distribution was observed, with what uncertainty, against which models; which accounts the result actually discriminated; and what new arrangement would make this account's prediction differ from its competitors'.

**Resolution discipline.** A distinction obtained through one kind of coupling is not promoted to a property of the substrate unless it persists across very different couplings.
