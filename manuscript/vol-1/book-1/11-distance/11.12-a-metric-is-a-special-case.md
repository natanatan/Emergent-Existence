# 11.12 A Metric Is a Special Case

We can now state the conditions under which the relational construction becomes a metric-like structure. On the relevant quotient domain, require non-negativity, zero-distance identity, and the triangle inequality. If reversible equivalence additionally yields symmetry, the quotient domain supports an ordinary metric.

▪ relational organization ⇝ metric axioms as consequences

The direction of explanation matters. Metric axioms should emerge from the organization rather than being used to define the organization in advance.
