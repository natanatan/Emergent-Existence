# 14.28 What Time Does Not Earn

Time still does not establish relativistic structure (a Lorentzian metric, invariant light speed, light cones and intervals, proper time, relative simultaneity, time dilation, gravitational redshift); any global time (cosmological time, a preferred foliation, a universal present); a thermodynamic arrow (entropy increase, microscopic irreversibility); or physical content (gravity, field equations, matter, quantum fields, energy, action, or the observed value of any physical constant).

It also does not establish that space and time are ontologically identical aspects of one metric structure. Book I has now earned a candidate spatial organization and a candidate temporal organization. Whether and how they become one physically constrained spacetime is a separate question.

This boundary is not a weakness to be concealed. It is the exact seam at which the constructive ontology becomes a physical research program.
