# 12.27 A Dimensionality Research Program

A serious model should begin without a fixed target dimension. Construct a relational substrate from previously earned ingredients. Allow its coupled relations to update or stabilize according to rules that do not contain coordinate triples, Euclidean adjacency, or a preferred three-dimensional embedding.

At several scale regimes σ, estimate the minimum number of independent collective modes required to preserve the declared metric relations, and measure the scaling dimension (12.19) over the same regimes. This yields a scale-dependent effective dimension d<sub>eff</sub>(σ).

▪ σ ↦ d<sub>eff</sub>(σ)

The model should be allowed to fail. The effective rank may grow, fluctuate, collapse, or vary by region, and the scaling dimension may settle near a noninteger value. Any of those outcomes is more informative than forcing the system toward three.

The particularly strong result would be spontaneous convergence toward a broad rank-three regime without the value three having been privileged in the rules:

▪ d<sub>eff</sub>(σ) → 3 over a broad stable regime

Such a result would not demonstrate physical space. It would show that low-dimensional geometric organization can arise from the candidate relational machinery without target dimensionality being inserted beforehand.
