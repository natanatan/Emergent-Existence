# 4.28 Precursors and Departures

**Precursors.** Shannon information (Shannon, 1948) supplies a quantitative theory of communication under probabilistic and coding assumptions not available here, and Landauer (1961) a later physical relation between logical erasure and energetic cost; neither is re-derived in Boundary. Constructor theory (Deutsch, 2013; Deutsch and Marletto, 2015) characterizes physical information through possible and impossible tasks on substrates. Its counterfactual orientation is a close neighbor to preservation under declared transformations, but it is a law-level physical framework, whereas Boundary is still pre-geometric and substrate-neutral.

**What is new here.** The chapter places frame-relative recoverability, retention, possibility, dependency precedence and criterion-based identity in one stage, between internally admissible succession and multi-relation organization, while withholding an anchor, orientation, metric time and physical measures of information. The claim under test is whether this placement and separation of roles can support the later chapters without circular inheritance.
