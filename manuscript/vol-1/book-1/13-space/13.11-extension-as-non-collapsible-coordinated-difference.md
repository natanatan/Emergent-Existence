# 13.11 Extension as Non-Collapsible Coordinated Difference

If A and B occupy non-equivalent localization classes, L(A) ≠ L(B), then there is spatial plurality. But not every plurality is yet extension.

Suppose collapsing the two classes, L(A), L(B) ↦ L*, destroys at least one declared spatial invariant H, so that H(Geo) ≠ H(Geo/L*). Their distinction is then structurally irreducible at that scale.

▪ extension = non-collapsible plurality of coordinated localization

Extension therefore does not have to be introduced as an independently existing continuum. It emerges because distinct spatial localization roles cannot be collapsed without changing the organization itself.
