# 4.3 Why Boundary Needs a Stronger Test

On the carrier {a,b}, the endomaps are id, the swap J<sub>𝒰</sub>, and two constant maps that collapse both inputs onto one image. id and J<sub>𝒰</sub> preserve dyadic separation. Constant maps erase it. If Boundary considered only 𝒰<sub>min</sub>, retention would be automatic; if it promoted every conceivable map into a physical update, it would add ontology without warrant.

The chapter therefore separates constitutive succession from diagnostic variation. The latter asks a robustness question about a structure already earned; it does not tell the primitive system what it may internally do.
