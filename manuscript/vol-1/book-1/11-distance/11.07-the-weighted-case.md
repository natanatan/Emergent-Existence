# 11.7 The Weighted Case

Equivalent steps are only the simplest case. A mature relational organization may contain mediating relations that differ in comparative magnitude.

w(r₁), w(r₂), …

If those weights are already grounded by the comparative machinery of Scale, then:

▪ L(κ) = Σ<sub>r ∈ κ</sub> w(r)

and distance becomes:

▪ d(A,B) = min<sub>κ ∈ K(A,B)</sub> Σ<sub>r ∈ κ</sub> w(r)

Weighted shortest-path form is familiar mathematics, but the mathematics is representational. The ontological burden lies beneath it: why are these relations the relevant mediators, why are their weights internally recoverable, why is their composition additive, why is the chosen scale standard legitimate, and does the result survive faithful re-description?

The weighted construction therefore remains Provisional until its weights can be derived from independently justified relational magnitude.
