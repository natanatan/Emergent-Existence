# 2.12 Could Difference Be Reduced Further?

One speculative possibility is that the dyad might eventually be reconstructed from recursive self-differentiation of a more primitive condition. That possibility is retained as a reduction hypothesis, not as part of the present derivation. The current architecture does not depend on its success.

A successful reduction would have to do more than rename the dyad. It would have to show how numerical two-ness and non-self relation arise without inserting them in equivalent form. Until such a construction exists, Proposition 2A should remain explicit about its primitive cost.
