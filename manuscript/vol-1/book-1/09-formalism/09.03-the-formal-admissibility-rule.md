# 9.3 The Formal Admissibility Rule

Formal Admissibility Rule. A symbol, operator, relation or construction may perform ontological work only when its content is inherited from earlier chapters or definable from inherited structure. Richer mathematics may be used externally for proof, simulation or bookkeeping, but its additional structure must not be silently read back into the modeled world.

The rule generalizes the discipline already used in Closure’s simulations. An external program may count iterations even when our ontology has no intrinsic clock. A researcher may store relations in a matrix package without granting our ontology a vector space.

▪ metalanguage richness ≠ object-language richness

From this chapter onward the book will freely use ordinary mathematics to study our model. The burden is to identify which parts of that mathematics are claims about our model itself.
