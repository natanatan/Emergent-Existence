# 20.28 Ontological Defensibility Pulse: Light

**Entities required.** Electromagnetic state, spatial relational neighborhood, update cycle, hand-off, open recursive closure, phase relation, recursive-return angular momentum, transverse handedness, coherence envelope, neighbor coupling, and a signed compatibility relation governing continuation.

**Ontological level.** Light appears at a higher-order dynamical level built from spatial geometry, energetic state and electromagnetic organization already earned.

**Lower-order dependencies.** Excitation, distinction, retention, transformation, recursive return, boundary, orientation, scale, distance, geometry, time, spatial neighborhoods, field-like organization, the invariant rate (Chapter 18) and the electric--magnetic structure of Chapter 19, including independence as zero overlap, transverse organization in the free-propagation regime, signed addition and mutual driving.

**Emergent causal properties.** Coherent propagation, directional persistence, phase recurrence, envelope formation and spreading, interference, polarization, reflection, refraction, forward and rotational push, delivery in units at interaction, and identity continuity through transformation.

**Claim type.** Relational, dynamical and mechanistic. The claim proposes a physical organization underlying established optical behavior; it does not claim to derive the values of c or h.

**New primitive test.** Phase, compatibility, recursive-return angular momentum, handedness and amplitude cannot remain unexplained labels. Each must be derived from earned structure or introduced as a new physical quantity with an explicit necessity, a unit and a test.

**Parsimony test.** One closure grammar should explain many luminous behaviors. If separate mechanisms are needed for propagation, interference, reflection, momentum and detection, the unification loses most of its value.

**Experimental discrimination.** The mature formalism must stay consistent with every measured relation and either supply new cross-regime constraints or produce new measurable consequences. Candidates named here: no frequency dependence of speed at any scale; delay near bodies with mass from denser medium activity (Chapter 22); a measurable signature of the recursive return's angular momentum.

**Resolution discipline.** Properties inferred from interaction must not automatically be promoted to intrinsic microscopic structure. In particular, the unit of energy observed at absorption is not promoted to a packet in transit. A proposed substrate feature earns ontological status only where it remains necessary across several independent interactions and resolutions.
