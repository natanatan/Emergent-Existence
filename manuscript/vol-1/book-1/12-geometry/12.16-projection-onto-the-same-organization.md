# 12.16 Projection Onto the Same Organization

There is no ontologically privileged screen onto which the world is projected. Probe, target, detector, and the relations that connect them are all internal to the same organization.

“Reality projecting onto itself” should therefore be read operationally: one bounded subsystem interacts with another, some distinctions survive the interaction and resolution process, and those surviving distinctions become available as a stable effective representation.

▪ measurement = relation between bounded subsystems of one relational organization

This formulation rejects both naive transparency and arbitrary subjectivism. What is resolved depends on substrate, frame, and interaction, but cross-frame invariants can still be real constraints imposed by the shared organization.

Objectivity is therefore relocated. It need not mean a view from nowhere. It can mean reproducibility of declared invariants across a sufficiently diverse family of admissible relational viewpoints.
