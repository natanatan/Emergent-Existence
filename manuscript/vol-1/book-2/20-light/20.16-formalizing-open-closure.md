# 20.16 Formalizing Open Closure

The qualitative structure needs a dynamical rule. Let $S_{i}$ be the organized state of the excitation in neighborhood $i$ and $N_{i}$ the relevant state of that neighborhood. The update cycle is

$$S_{i}^{*} =  U\left( S_{i},\, N_{i} \right)$$

where U is the update rule and $S_{i}^{*}$ is the completed state. The hand-off is

$$S_{i + 1} = \mathcal{R}\left( S_{i}^{*},\, N_{i + 1},\, H \right)$$

where $\mathcal{R}$ is the re-instantiation relation and H stands for constraints inherited from the surrounding spatial and electromagnetic organization. The propagation rate is set by the whole sequence, U applied as many times as the local cycles require and then $\mathcal{R}$ (20.12); one application of U is not one spatial step.

A viable formalism must state at least four criteria: when the update cycle counts as complete; how neighboring relations are recruited into the envelope; what degree of compatibility is enough for continuation; and when continuity has failed strongly enough that the original excitation no longer persists. These criteria turn the picture of open closure into a construction that can be tested.
