# 4.1 Where Distinction Leaves Us

Chapter 3 gives an operationally nontrivial dyad. Every application of 𝒰<sub>min</sub> exchanges the active configuration, and the induced map satisfies J<sub>𝒰</sub>² = id. Nothing stands still, yet reciprocal non-equivalence is never lost under that update because the update is generated from the same exchange-symmetric structure.

Boundary begins where that guarantee ends. The relevant question is not whether a differs from b under J<sub>𝒰</sub>. It is whether the operative distinction remains recoverable when its realization, carrier, or surrounding structure is varied, and whether retaining it can make a difference to what follows.
