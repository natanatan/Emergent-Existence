# 2.3 Proposition 2A: Primitive Difference

Proposition 2A - primitive difference. The least constructive structure beyond the unresolved Origin is a symmetric dyad whose two poles are numerically two as a primitive cost and are related by a symmetric, irreflexive relation of non-equivalence. Difference and its poles are co-constitutive: neither independently constituted relata nor a relation existing prior to all relata is asserted.

▪ 𝔇 = ⟨a, b ; ≬⟩

Here 𝔇 names the whole primitive dyad. The symbols a and b are metalanguage labels used so the structure can be discussed. They do not yet name objects with intrinsic properties or states carrying histories.

▪ a ≬ b ⟺ b ≬ a

▪ ¬(a ≬ a) and ¬(b ≬ b)

Symmetry preserves exchange equivalence. Irreflexivity prevents the non-equivalence relation from applying to a pole with itself. Neither condition introduces a metric or a magnitude of difference.

▪ The proposition states its primitive cost rather than deriving multiplicity from weak discernibility.

Why any dyadic Difference exists at all remains outside what this chapter can derive.
