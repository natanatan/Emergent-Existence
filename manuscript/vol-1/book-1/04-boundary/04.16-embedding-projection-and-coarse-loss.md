# 4.16 Embedding, Projection, and Coarse Loss

A useful nontrivial test can embed the dyad into a larger carrier X without yet claiming that X is an integrated relational domain:

▪ e : 𝒞<sub>𝔇</sub> → X

If e(a) and e(b) remain resolvable as non-equivalent under W, the distinction survives enlargement. X is only a target of the robustness test.

A many-to-one projection can do the opposite. It may preserve some distinctions while collapsing others. Boundary needs only the formal possibility of such maps; Scale will later earn physically grounded coarse-graining criteria. The present chapter must not smuggle metric smallness or resolution scale into the projection.
