# 11.15 Local Distance Does Not Guarantee Global Geometry

A domain may support valid pairwise distances without those distances composing into one globally coherent geometry. Different regions may employ different relational standards. Some may contain directed asymmetries. Others may possess disconnected components or incompatible scale calibrations.

▪ local metric structure ⇏ global geometry

This gives us another important boundary. Distance asks whether separation can acquire magnitude. Geometry will ask whether a sufficiently coherent family of such relations can be represented as a geometry.
