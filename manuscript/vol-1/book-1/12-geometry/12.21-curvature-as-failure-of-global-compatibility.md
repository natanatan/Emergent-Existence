# 12.21 Curvature as Failure of Global Compatibility

Local geometric coherence does not guarantee global uniformity. Imagine carrying a local comparison standard through a sequence of overlapping neighborhoods and returning to a relationally equivalent starting region. If the returned relation depends on the route taken, the local standards fail to compose into one globally path-independent comparison.

The mature mathematics of differential geometry represents this with connections, parallel transport, and curvature. The present chapter needs only the relational core: curvature-like structure appears when local geometric standards are individually coherent but cannot all be made mutually compatible by one global identification.

▪ curvature-like structure = persistent failure of local geometric standards to remain globally compatible

This should not replace the mathematical definition of curvature. It identifies the kind of relational failure that the later formalism is expected to encode.

Curvature is therefore not introduced as a substance or as a bend visible only from outside an embedding. It is an intrinsic higher-order relation among local metric organizations.
