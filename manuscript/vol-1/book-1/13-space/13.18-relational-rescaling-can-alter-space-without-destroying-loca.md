# 13.18 Relational Rescaling Can Alter Space Without Destroying Locality

Spatial coordination also allows a distinction between local structural persistence and global metric persistence. Suppose local neighborhoods remain approximately invariant while larger-scale separations change.

▪ N<sub>n</sub>(A) ~ N<sub>n+1</sub>(A), while d<sub>n+1</sub>(A,B) \> d<sub>n</sub>(A,B)

A localization role can remain identifiable, L<sub>n</sub>(A) ~ L<sub>n+1</sub>(A), without preserving every metric relation to distant loci.

▪ persistence of localization ≠ persistence of global metric separation

This preserves a conceptual route by which later cosmological expansion could be interpreted as evolving relational scale rather than motion through an external container. That interpretation is not derived here. Space merely keeps the possibility structurally available.
