# 10.9 Magnitude Is Not Yet Proportion

We can say equal, greater and lesser. We still cannot say by what factor one magnitude differs from another.

▪ A ≻ B does not yet give mag(A)/mag(B) = r

This is easy to miss because ordinary arithmetic supplies ratios the moment it supplies numbers. Here the two arrive separately, and the ground between them must be crossed on foot. Ordering magnitudes is weaker than comparing them by a stable proportion. Proportion is what the rest of we earn.
